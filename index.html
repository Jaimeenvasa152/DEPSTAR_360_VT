Welcome to DEPSTAR

Devang Patel Institute of Advanced Technology and Research (DEPSTAR), a constituent of CHARUSAT, is managed by a diverse group of experts including technocrats, educators, and industry professionals. Supported by IPCO Industries and led by philanthropist Shri Devang Patel, the institute aims to excel in advanced technology education and research. It focuses on building strong infrastructure, experienced faculty, global collaborations, and modern processes. DEPSTAR offers 300 seats across Computer Engineering (120), Computer Science & Engineering (120), and Information Technology (60), with faculty averaging over 6 years of experience in teaching, industry, and research.

Thank you for visiting DEPSTAR

Sophos Network Security Lab

State-of-the-art infrastructure dedicated to network security operations, ethical hacking, and cyber threat intelligence. Powered by Sophos, this lab provides students with hands-on experience in fighting modern cyber threats.

Advanced Mac Lab

Equipped with state-of-the-art Apple iMacs featuring M-series Apple Silicon. Dedicated to mobile app development, AR/VR engineering, and high-performance creative workloads.

AR/VR Innovation Lab

Equipped with Meta Quest headsets and spatial computing infrastructure. Students build immersive realities, simulation software, and next-generation interactive experiences.

AI/ML Research Lab

Powered by high-performance GPU clusters, this lab focuses on deep learning, neural networks, natural language processing, and advanced machine learning modeling.

Interactive Classroom

Our classrooms are designed for collaborative learning,high-speed WiFi, and ergonomically designed seating to foster an engaging environment for 120+ students.
